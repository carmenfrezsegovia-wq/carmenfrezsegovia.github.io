# About the Designer
**Carmen Segovia**

I spent my career doing the "safe" thing, but I learned that stability can be a prison if your ideas are too bold for the industry. I am a fashion designer who values authenticity more than comfort, and the raw and real more than the shiny and fake.

I believe we can thrive in style and vibrant color without creating a wasteland of polyester. I am an **Antifashionista** who believes in clean waters and a healthy nature. This is why I have taken an oath against plastics and polyester in my textiles.

The sustainability discussion has become a farce when corporations label synthetic plastics as "sustainable" alternatives to wool, cotton, or hemp. Unlike plastic, plant fibers return to the earth as healthy soil for our natural habitat.

My work is a reflection of these values. **Copy-Paste** is my "New World"—a space where craftsmanship, sustainability, and circularity are the priorities. This is my rebellion against the fast-fashion industry, changing it and slowing it down, one drop at a time.
