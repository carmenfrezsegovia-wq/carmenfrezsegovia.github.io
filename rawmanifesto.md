# The Raw Manifesto: Copy-Paste
**"A rebellion against the fast-fashion monster."**

In today’s fashion industry, everything is **Copy-Paste**. It is a world of vanity—a dark entity that prioritizes cheap production and mass-market speed over human life and the health of our planet. We chose this name as an act of defiance. While the world "pastes" polyester into landfills, we are reclaiming the soul of the garment through **Slow Fashion**.

* **The Plant-Fiber Oath**: We have taken a vow to never use polyester; we work exclusively with original plant materials like **Cotton and Hemp**.
* **Natural Minimalism**: Our minimalism is found in the purity of our fibers and our total rejection of plastics.
* **Vibrancy & Evolution**: In our colors, we celebrate the energy of the natural world. We embrace the fact that natural dyes fade and evolve over time.
* **Circularity in Color**: To support a circular life for your garment, we offer non-toxic dyes so you can refresh the original shade or transform your top into a new color whenever you choose.
* **Unprocessed & Unpolished**: We celebrate the raw and the real. Our aesthetic is the opposite of the dull, synthetic "perfection" of modern minimalism.
