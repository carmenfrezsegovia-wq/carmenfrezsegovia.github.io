# Brand-Identity-Copy-Paste.md

## I. Antigravity Global Style Guide Prompt
**Role**: High-End Fashion Creative Director  
**Brand Name**: Copy Paste  
**Slogan**: "Raw is the New Real"

**Visual Identity & Layout**:
* **The High-End Scrapbook**: Design the homepage as a curated fashion mood board with non-linear, overlapping elements that feel intentional and expensive, never DIY.
* **The Surreal Contrast**: Layer monochrome (black and white) product imagery over hyper-colorful, vivid floral backgrounds inspired by Frida Kahlo.
* **The Material Focus**: Visual textures must highlight the raw beauty of plant fibers—Cotton and Hemp—to reinforce the zero-microplastic brand oath.

**Color Constraints**:
* **Primary Anchor**: Mahogany Red (`#7B1113`).
* **The Black Ban**: Strictly no black (`#000000`) UI elements or text. Use **Mahogany Red** or **Deep Moss Green** for dark contrast.
* **The White Rule**: No clinical or blue-toned whites. All neutral spaces must be warm **Ivory**, **Cream**, or **Bone**.
* **Vibe**: Bold color explosions of Yellows and Pinks against a grounded Mahogany base.

**Typography**:
* Use sophisticated, clean headers for the brand name **Copy Paste**.
* Integrate subtle "handwritten" style accents for captions to capture the "loner artist" soul and the scrapbook aesthetic.

## II. Antigravity Content Prompt: The Manifesto Section

**Section Type**: High-End Hero/Manifesto Section  
**Visual Style**: Asymmetric layout, "torn diary" aesthetic.

**Copy Strategy**:
* **Main Headline**: **COPY PASTE.** Because the original is already a replica of nature.
* **Sub-Headline**: We named it **Copy Paste** because we refuse to do either. In a world of digital mass-production and polyester clones, we choose the "Raw."
* **The Core Pillars**:
    * **No Black.** (Because the soul isn't a void). 
    * **No Plastic.** (Because the Earth shouldn't pay for your outfit). 
    * **No Perfection.** (Because every stitch of our plant-fiber crochet is a one-of-a-kind error turned into art).
* **The Oath**: High-end plant fibers. Cotton and Hemp only. Zero microplastics. One hundred percent real.

**Formatting Instructions**:
* Render "COPY PASTE" in oversized **Mahogany Red** typography.
* Background: A surreal floral collage (Frida Kahlo inspired) blended with a high-resolution texture of raw hemp fiber.
